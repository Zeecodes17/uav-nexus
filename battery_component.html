Drone Battery

What is a Drone Battery?

Most drones use LiPo (Lithium Polymer) batteries to provide high power output and lightweight energy storage.

Key Parameters

  • Cell count (3S, 4S, 6S)
  • Capacity (mAh)
  • C-rating (discharge capability)

Typical Use Cases

  • 3S–4S for small drones
  • 4S–6S for racing and medium drones
  • 6S+ for heavy-lift drones

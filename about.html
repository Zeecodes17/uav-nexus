About This App

A structured engineering reference for drone design, operation, and safety decision-making.

Purpose

This application is designed to support engineering-driven thinking in unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) design, mission planning, and emergency handling. It emphasizes understanding trade-offs, margins, and consequences rather than providing automated or firmware-specific solutions.

Design Philosophy

  • Firmware-agnostic and platform-independent
  • Offline-friendly and deterministic
  • Focused on reasoning, not automation
  • Action-oriented guidance for emergency scenarios

The app intentionally avoids live telemetry, configuration tutorials, or manufacturer-specific behavior.

Scope & Limitations

  • Design-time estimation and planning tools only
  • Emergency guidance is advisory, not prescriptive
  • Does not replace flight controller fail-safes
  • Does not provide real-time flight data

All calculations and guidance are based on conservative, simplified engineering models.

Intended Audience

  • Engineering students and researchers
  • UAV hobbyists seeking deeper understanding
  • Competition teams and academic projects
  • Pilots interested in structured safety thinking

Safety Disclaimer

This application does not provide flight authorization, legal compliance validation, or real-time safety assurance. The user is solely responsible for safe operation and adherence to local aviation regulations.

Always perform independent checks and follow applicable rules before operating any UAV.

The goal of this app is not to replace pilot judgment, but to strengthen it through structured thinking.

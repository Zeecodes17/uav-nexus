ESC ↔ Motor Wiring

Scope

This page covers the physical wiring between an Electronic Speed Controller (ESC) and a brushless motor, including connection order, direction rules, and verification steps before proceeding further.

Prerequisites

  • Motors mounted securely on the frame
  • ESCs mounted and powered from PDB or power source
  • Flight controller installed (not armed)
  • Propellers removed

Wiring Steps

  1. Identify the three output wires from the ESC
  2. Identify the three phase wires from the motor
  3. Connect the ESC wires to the motor wires in any order
  4. Ensure all connectors or solder joints are secure
  5. Route wires away from rotating parts and sharp edges

At this stage, motor rotation direction does not matter. Direction will be verified and corrected during testing.

Motor Direction Rules

  • Multirotor drones require alternating motor directions
  • Motor direction is determined by phase order, not wire color
  • Correct direction depends on motor position on the frame

To reverse motor direction, swap any two of the three ESC–motor wires. No ESC reprogramming is required.

Connection Methods

  • Bullet connectors: Easier motor replacement and quick direction changes
  • Direct soldering: Lower resistance, preferred for high-current or permanent builds

Verification Checklist

  • All motor mounting screws tightened
  • No exposed copper on motor connections
  • Wires secured and strain-relieved
  • Motor shafts spin freely without obstruction

Common Faults & Fixes

  • Motor spins in wrong direction: Swap any two motor wires
  • Motor does not spin: Check ESC power input and signal connection
  • Motor vibrates or stutters: Inspect loose connectors or poor solder joints

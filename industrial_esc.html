Industrial ESC

What is an Industrial ESC?

Industrial ESCs are high-power motor controllers used in heavy-lift and commercial drones. They are designed for reliability, high current handling, and long-duration operations.

Key Features

  • High current ratings (60A–200A+)
  • Supports high-voltage batteries
  • Improved thermal protection
  • Stable operation for large motors

Typical Use Cases

  • Delivery drones
  • Agriculture UAVs
  • Heavy-lift camera drones
  • Industrial inspection platforms
